A simple, four-step process.

Every engagement is focused, efficient and built around your goals.

Book a Free Consultation
01

Understand

Understand your business requirements.

We begin with a conversation about your business: what you do, what is working, what is not, and what you want to achieve.

You

  • Tell us about your business and goals
  • Share any urgent deadlines or problems
  • Mention tools and systems you already use

BIZYNQ

  • Ask the right questions
  • Review your current setup
  • Identify what needs attention first
02

Recommend

Recommend the right services and solutions.

We suggest a clear set of services matched to your stage and budget, with scope and pricing explained up front.

You

  • Review our recommendation and quote
  • Ask questions and adjust the scope
  • Approve what you want to go ahead with

BIZYNQ

  • Explain options in plain language
  • Prioritise what is essential now
  • Share a clear scope and timeline
03

Execute

Execute and implement the solutions.

Once agreed, we get to work, whether that is filings, a website, automation or dashboards, and keep you updated.

You

  • Provide documents and access when needed
  • Review progress and give feedback
  • Approve final deliverables

BIZYNQ

  • Carry out the agreed work
  • Keep you informed at every step
  • Hand over with clear guidance
04

Support

Provide continued support and assistance.

Business needs change. We stay available for questions, updates and the next stage of your growth.

You

  • Reach us when you have questions
  • Tell us when your needs change
  • Add services as you grow

BIZYNQ

  • Respond to queries
  • Help with recurring requirements
  • Recommend next steps when relevant
